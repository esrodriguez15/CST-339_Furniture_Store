About DEA's Furntiture Store

DEA's Furniture store was created with the idea to be a one stop source for all of your furniture needs. The creators, Damian, Esmeralda, and Angelo believe in making it an easy experience to manage inventory as employees and view products as customers.

Being Grand Canyon University student has given us the opportunity to come together to create this application for you. Thank you for choosing DEA! Please reach out if there any questions!
